Practise the conversation, not the breach. Counterpart puts you across from a simulated adversary — some scenarios train you to give away nothing, others train you to unmask a deepfake by asking the right questions.

Each app is a single self-contained HTML file. Nothing you type is transmitted anywhere; all scoring runs locally.

Scenarios in v2

  • 🎯 The Friendly Quiz — a quiz bot harvests security-question answers one fragment at a time.
  • 🪤 The Sieve — yes/no games where saying no leaks the complement just as surely as yes.
  • 🛡️ The Admirer — the grooming-and-coercion ladder used in sextortion. Non-explicit by design.
  • 🧩 The Pincer — two strangers in parallel chats fuse harmless fragments into one identity.
  • 🎙️ The Voice Note — voice-message extortion: a cloned “recording of you”, and a push to reply by voice.
  • 🎭 The Impersonator — a deepfake of someone you know makes an urgent request. You are the verifier.

Every scenario ships multiple scripts and picks one at random, so no two runs are the same.

Safety note. These are training simulations. The scripted adversaries never request or display explicit content. If a real situation resembles these scenarios — especially one involving a minor — stop and reach a trusted adult and the reporting resources shown at the end of each run.
